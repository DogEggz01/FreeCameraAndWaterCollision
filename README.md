# FreeCameraAndWaterCollision
- Water Collision default on, can be toggle off in configurator.
- You can adjust the camera collision in configurator if needed (For example better water clearance in rough waves)
- Free Camera Mode have double the vanilla distance(can move furthur away from boat)
- Press B in 3rd person camera mode to enter/leave Free Camera mode.
- WASD to move around, Space for raise vertically, Ctrl to down vertically
- Mouse Scroll to zoom in and out
- Hold Shift to increase camera moving speed by 1.5x
- Press H to detach Camera from boat. Press H again to attach the camera to boat again.
